About me

Built by Faith. Driven by Purpose. Learning to Create.

I’m Vincent Ferreras, a Computer Science student at City College of Calamba and a servant leader who believes that what we learn becomes more meaningful when we use it to help others.

My story started not with a keyboard, but with the church choir. Since 2014, I’ve had the opportunity to serve in different ministries, lead, teach, and work with people in church, school, and the community. Through these experiences, I’ve learned that leadership is not always about being in front. Sometimes, it simply means being willing to step up, lend a hand, and serve wherever you are needed.

Now, I’m discovering how my passion for technology can become part of that journey too. As a Computer Science student, I’m learning how code, design, and creativity can turn ideas into something useful and meaningful.

This website is a small reflection of that journey. Faith gives me my foundation. Technology gives me a way to create. Service gives me a reason to use both.

I’m still learning, still building, and still figuring things out along the way. But I want to make the most of what God has given me, keep growing, and use what I learn to make a meaningful impact on the people around me.

This site is my Web Design and Programming (CS211) project, built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
