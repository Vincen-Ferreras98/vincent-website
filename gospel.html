How to be saved

“For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God.” Ephesians 2:8 KJV

Salvation is not something we earn through good works, religion, church membership or personal goodness. It is the gift of God, made possible through the death, burial and resurrection of Jesus Christ. Here is what the Word of God says.

You can have eternal life

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.” John 3:16 KJV

Salvation is not only about becoming a better person. It is about being reconciled to God through Jesus Christ and receiving the gift of eternal life. Our assurance rests in Jesus Christ and His promise, not in our own ability (John 10:28).

The Gospel in one message

  • I am a sinner.
  • My sin has a penalty.
  • Jesus Christ died for my sins and rose again.
  • I cannot save myself through good works.
  • I must put my faith in Jesus Christ.
  • God promises eternal life to those who believe in Him.

A prayer of faith

If you understand the Gospel and have put your faith in Jesus Christ, you can speak to God in prayer. No special words are required, so pray sincerely from your heart. For example:

“Lord Jesus, I know that I am a sinner and that I cannot save myself. I believe that You died on the cross for my sins, that You were buried, and that You rose again. I put my faith and trust in You alone as my Lord and Saviour. Please forgive my sins and save me. Thank You for Your love and for the gift of eternal life. In Jesus Christ’s name, Amen.”

Remember: the prayer itself does not save you. Jesus Christ saves you. Prayer is how you express your faith and trust in Him.

Jesus Christ is the only way

“Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.” John 14:6 KJV

If you have trusted Jesus Christ as your Saviour, your next step is to grow in His Word, pray, follow Him and make Him known to others (Matthew 28:19-20).
